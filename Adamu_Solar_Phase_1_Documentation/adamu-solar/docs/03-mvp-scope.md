# Adamu Solar Website --- MVP Scope

**Document ID:** AS-SCOPE-001\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Draft for client validation

## 1. MVP objective

Launch a professional bilingual website that explains Adamu's confirmed
solar services, demonstrates genuine work, and makes it easy for
prospective customers to contact him.

## 2. Included in the MVP

### Five core pages

1.  **Home** --- headline, value proposition, key services, selected
    projects, trust content, calls to action.
2.  **About** --- biography, experience, qualifications, and
    professional approach.
3.  **Services** --- confirmed service descriptions and inquiry calls to
    action.
4.  **Projects** --- approved photos and short descriptions of completed
    work.
5.  **Contact** --- phone, WhatsApp, verified social links, service
    coverage, and inquiry form.

### Shared capabilities

-   English and Amharic content for all five pages.
-   Responsive header, navigation, footer, and language selector.
-   Direct phone and WhatsApp links.
-   Inquiry form connected to a confirmed delivery destination.
-   Basic SEO metadata and social sharing metadata.
-   Accessible labels, keyboard navigation, and image alt text.
-   Privacy notice for inquiry submissions.
-   Responsive layouts and compressed images.
-   Deployment on an agreed hosting platform.
-   README, launch checklist, and basic handover notes.

## 3. Explicitly out of scope for MVP

-   Online store, cart, checkout, or payment processing.
-   Customer accounts, sign-in, or user dashboards.
-   Admin dashboard or content management system.
-   Database-backed project management.
-   Automated quotations or solar system sizing calculator.
-   Real-time inventory or product pricing synchronization.
-   Appointment scheduling and booking calendar.
-   Customer relationship management (CRM) integrations.
-   Blog or news publishing system.
-   Multi-branch company management.
-   Paid advertising management.
-   Advanced analytics or conversion tracking beyond basic setup if
    agreed.
-   Claims of guaranteed energy savings, warranties, or service coverage
    without evidence.

These features may be considered later if customer needs justify them.

## 4. Page-level content baseline

### Home

-   Brand name and clear headline.
-   Brief explanation of services and location.
-   Primary WhatsApp/contact call to action.
-   Service cards.
-   Selected project previews.
-   Trust content based on verified experience or qualifications.
-   Footer and social links.

### About

-   Approved portrait and biography.
-   Professional background and relevant certificates.
-   Working approach and confirmed service area.

### Services

-   One section per confirmed service.
-   What the service includes.
-   Appropriate customer type or use case.
-   Contact action to ask for a consultation or quote.

### Projects

-   Genuine photos supplied or approved by Adamu.
-   Short, factual descriptions.
-   Location and customer details only with permission.
-   A clear way to inquire about similar work.

### Contact

-   Phone and WhatsApp actions.
-   Contact form.
-   Service area and hours if confirmed.
-   Verified Facebook and Telegram links.
-   Brief privacy notice.

## 5. Release strategy

**Release 1 --- MVP:** Five-page bilingual marketing website and working
inquiries.

**Release 2 --- Optional improvements:** Blog, richer product catalog,
content management, analytics, or CRM only after reviewing real usage
and client priorities.

**Release 3 --- Company growth:** If Adamu establishes a company,
consider updated branding, team profiles, company credentials,
additional service locations, and more advanced lead management.

## 6. Dependencies

The MVP cannot be considered launch-ready until: - Adamu approves the
factual content. - Required photos and credentials are supplied or
intentionally omitted. - Permission is confirmed for testimonials and
customer/project images. - Phone and WhatsApp numbers are verified. -
The form submission destination is chosen and tested. - Amharic content
is reviewed. - Hosting and domain decisions are agreed.

## 7. Scope change rule

Any feature not listed as included must be discussed before
implementation. Record the reason, effort, cost or recurring service
implications, and effect on the launch date before adding it to the MVP.
