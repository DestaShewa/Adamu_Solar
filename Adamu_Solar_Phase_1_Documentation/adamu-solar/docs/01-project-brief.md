# Adamu Solar Website --- Project Brief

**Document ID:** AS-PB-001\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Draft for client validation\
**Phase:** 1 --- Requirements and Planning\
**Last updated:** 15 October 2026

## 1. Project overview

Adamu Solar is a professional online presence for Adamu Shewa, a solar
professional based in Arba Minch, Ethiopia. The first release will
present his services, experience, completed projects, credentials, and
contact options in a clear, trustworthy, mobile-friendly website.

The website will support Adamu's current personal brand. It should also
be structured so that the brand can later grow into a company website
without requiring an unnecessary rebuild.

## 2. Problem and opportunity

Potential customers may need help finding a solar professional,
understanding available services, checking examples of completed work,
and contacting someone for advice or a quote. A focused website can
bring this information together and support promotion through Facebook,
Telegram, WhatsApp, and other channels.

These are project assumptions to validate with Adamu, not measured
market findings.

## 3. Vision

Build a trustworthy, accessible online presence that helps people in
Arba Minch and confirmed surrounding service areas understand Adamu's
solar services and contact him easily.

## 4. Project objectives

1.  Present Adamu Shewa and his solar services professionally.
2.  Make service information available in English and Amharic.
3.  Showcase genuine project photographs, credentials, and customer
    testimonials where permission is granted.
4.  Make phone and WhatsApp contact straightforward.
5.  Provide an inquiry form with clear submission feedback.
6.  Support basic search-engine discovery and sharing on social media.
7.  Deliver a responsive website that Adamu can maintain with simple
    documented updates.

## 5. Target audiences

-   Households considering solar installation or maintenance.
-   Churches and other community institutions.
-   Small and medium businesses.
-   NGOs and other organizations, where services and procurement
    requirements are suitable.
-   Other customers seeking solar consultation or products.

## 6. Proposed services to present

Subject to confirmation of Adamu's actual capabilities and available
products:

-   Solar system installation.
-   Solar system maintenance and troubleshooting.
-   Solar system design.
-   Solar consultation.
-   Solar product sales.

Do not publish products, technical capabilities, warranties, prices,
certifications, or service guarantees until verified with Adamu.

## 7. Scope summary

The MVP consists of five pages:

1.  Home
2.  About Adamu
3.  Services
4.  Projects
5.  Contact

Shared site elements include navigation, language switching, footer,
contact calls to action, basic SEO metadata, and responsive layouts.

## 8. Deliverables

-   Approved project brief and requirements.
-   Agreed MVP scope and acceptance criteria.
-   Brand and content assets supplied or approved by Adamu.
-   Five-page bilingual website.
-   Working phone and WhatsApp links.
-   Inquiry form and a functioning submission destination.
-   Responsive and cross-browser checks.
-   Production deployment and handover instructions.
-   Git repository and project README.

## 9. Success measures

Initial launch checks:

-   All five pages are available in English and Amharic.
-   All published contact links use client-approved details.
-   The inquiry form successfully delivers submissions to an agreed
    destination and shows success/error feedback.
-   No placeholder testimonials, invented credentials, or unverified
    claims remain.
-   Main user journeys work on mobile and desktop.
-   No known critical defects remain at launch.

Longer-term business indicators to review after launch:

-   Number of relevant inquiries received.
-   Number of inquiries that become consultations or quotes.
-   Traffic sources and frequently visited pages.
-   Engagement with project and service content.

Traffic and lead targets should be set after establishing a baseline; do
not promise a specific number of customers.

## 10. Constraints and assumptions

-   The initial website is a marketing and inquiry website, not an
    e-commerce platform.
-   No database, customer account system, admin dashboard, or payment
    processing is required for the MVP.
-   The developer is building the website without charging a development
    fee; domain, paid services, maintenance, and other expenses must be
    agreed separately.
-   Adamu will supply and approve business information, photographs,
    testimonials, credentials, contact details, and translations.
-   A custom domain is optional and may have a separate recurring cost.
-   The inquiry form requires an agreed delivery method. A visual form
    alone is not considered functional.
-   Service coverage beyond Arba Minch must be confirmed before
    publication.

## 11. Stakeholders and responsibilities

**Adamu Shewa --- Client / subject-matter owner** - Confirms services,
experience, qualifications, service area, business contact details, and
claims. - Supplies or approves content and images. - Approves design and
public launch. - Confirms permission to publish customer testimonials
and project images.

**Developer** - Documents requirements and scope. - Designs, builds,
tests, deploys, and documents the website. - Protects credentials and
avoids publishing private customer information. - Explains hosting,
domain, maintenance, and update responsibilities.

## 12. Approval required

Before Phase 2, Adamu should approve: - Service list and coverage
area. - Public phone and WhatsApp number. - English and Amharic
content. - Photos, certificates, and testimonial permissions. - Inquiry
form destination. - MVP scope and any recurring costs.
