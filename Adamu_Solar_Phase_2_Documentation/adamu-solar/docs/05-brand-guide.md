# Adamu Solar --- Brand Guide

**Document ID:** AS-BRAND-001\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Proposed baseline for client approval\
**Phase:** 2 --- Brand Identity and UI/UX Design

## 1. Brand foundation

**Brand name:** Adamu Solar\
**Professional:** Adamu Shewa\
**Location:** Arba Minch, Ethiopia; surrounding service areas to be
confirmed.

**Brand positioning:** A local solar professional offering confirmed
installation, maintenance, system design, consultation, and solar
product services.

**Primary audience:** Households, churches, small businesses,
institutions, and organizations seeking solar-related services.

**Brand personality:** Trustworthy, practical, knowledgeable,
approachable, safety-conscious, and professional.

**Brand promise:** Make it easier for customers to understand Adamu's
solar services, review genuine work, and request a conversation or
quote.

## 2. Approved design direction

-   Logo concept: sun + solar panel + leaf, paired with the ADAMU SOLAR
    wordmark.
-   Hero image: a real, client-approved solar installation photograph,
    preferably from Adamu's own work.
-   Tagline: **Reliable Solar Solutions for Homes, Churches and
    Businesses.**
-   Language control: compact **EN \| አማ** toggle.
-   Visual style: modern professional, clear layouts, strong
    photography, restrained decoration, readable typography.
-   Overall approach: mobile-first and trust-focused, not a busy or
    overly animated marketing template.

The logo concept and tagline are design choices. The final logo artwork
must be created and approved before implementation.

## 4. Typography

Recommended font pairing: - **Headings:** Poppins, with a suitable
fallback. - **Body and UI:** Inter, with a suitable fallback. -
**Amharic:** Choose and test a font with complete Ethiopic glyph
support, such as Noto Sans Ethiopic, and verify the visual pairing
in-browser.

Typography rules: - Use clear hierarchy: one primary page heading,
meaningful section headings, then body copy. - Body text should be
comfortable to read on phones. - Avoid long all-caps paragraphs. - Use
line-height and spacing generously. - Do not shrink Amharic text to fit;
adjust layout instead.

## 5. Logo usage

The logo should combine a simple sun, solar-panel geometry, and a leaf
motif with the ADAMU SOLAR wordmark.

Required variants: 1. Full-color horizontal logo for light backgrounds.
2. White/reversed logo for dark backgrounds. 3. Standalone symbol for
favicon and compact spaces.

Rules: - Preserve proportions and clear space. - Do not stretch, skew,
add unapproved shadows, or change colors arbitrarily. - Ensure the
symbol remains recognizable at small sizes. - Keep a text-based fallback
if the logo image fails to load. - Do not use a generated or stock logo
as if it were a trademark registration or a certified mark.

## 6. Photography and imagery

Priority order: 1. Adamu's real project photos with permission. 2.
Adamu's approved professional portrait. 3. Genuine product/equipment
photos that represent products he can supply. 4. Licensed or
appropriately permitted supporting imagery, clearly not presented as
Adamu's own work.

Photo guidelines: - Use high-quality, well-lit images. - Crop
consistently and keep important equipment or people visible. - Compress
images and provide suitable alternative text. - Obtain permission before
publishing customer premises, identifiable people, testimonials, or
sensitive site details. - Do not imply that stock imagery represents a
completed Adamu project.

## 7. Iconography and visual elements

-   Use one consistent line-icon family.
-   Prefer simple solar, tool, consultation, product, location, phone,
    and message icons.
-   Avoid decorative icons that do not add meaning.
-   Use restrained borders, subtle shadows, consistent corner radii, and
    generous spacing.
-   Animation should be minimal and must not block content or
    interaction.

## 8. Voice and writing

Tone: direct, respectful, practical, locally relevant, and clear.

Preferred examples: - "Request a solar consultation." - "Explore our
solar services." - "Ask about a solar installation." - "Contact Adamu to
discuss your project."

Avoid: - Unsupported claims such as "best in Ethiopia." - Guaranteed
savings, uptime, or performance without evidence. - Unverified
certifications, warranties, prices, customer counts, or years of
experience. - Claims of nationwide service unless confirmed.

## 9. Tagline and key message

**Primary tagline:** Reliable Solar Solutions for Homes, Churches and
Businesses.

Supporting copy should explain what Adamu actually offers and how to
contact him. Translate the tagline into Amharic naturally and have the
translation reviewed before publication.

-   [ ] Consistent typography and icon style.
