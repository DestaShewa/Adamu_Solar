# Adamu Solar --- Phase 2 Design Acceptance Criteria

**Document ID:** AS-DESIGN-AC-001\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Pending client sign-off

Phase 2 is complete when the brand and UI/UX baseline is documented,
reviewed, and approved. These criteria do not require code
implementation.

## 1. Brand guide

-   [ ] **D-01:** Brand name is consistently written as "Adamu Solar."
-   [ ] **D-02:** The logo direction uses a sun + solar panel + leaf
    concept and wordmark.
-   [ ] **D-03:** Full-color, reversed, and icon-only logo variants are
    specified.
-   [ ] **D-04:** Primary navy, green, amber accent, background, and
    text colors are documented.
-   [ ] **D-05:** Typography recommendations include a suitable Ethiopic
    font for Amharic.
-   [ ] **D-06:** Photography rules distinguish Adamu's real projects
    from generic supporting imagery.
-   [ ] **D-07:** Brand voice and examples of claims to avoid are
    documented.
-   [ ] **D-08:** The approved tagline is "Reliable Solar Solutions for
    Homes, Churches and Businesses," subject to final content review.

## 2. Sitemap and page specifications

-   [ ] **D-09:** The sitemap contains exactly five core pages: Home,
    About, Services, Projects, Contact.
-   [ ] **D-10:** Each page has a defined purpose and section order.
-   [ ] **D-11:** Each page includes a clear next action for the
    visitor.
-   [ ] **D-12:** Header, footer, navigation, and language switching
    behavior are defined.
-   [ ] **D-13:** The inquiry form fields, validation states,
    success/error states, and privacy notice are specified.
-   [ ] **D-14:** English and Amharic coverage is defined for all pages
    and shared UI.
-   [ ] **D-15:** No e-commerce, accounts, admin dashboard, or other
    excluded feature is silently added.

## 3. Responsive and accessible design rules

-   [ ] **D-16:** Mobile, tablet, and desktop layout behavior is
    documented.
-   [ ] **D-17:** Responsive behavior is defined for navigation, hero,
    cards, gallery, and form.
-   [ ] **D-18:** Keyboard access, focus visibility, semantic headings,
    contrast, and alt text rules are documented.
-   [ ] **D-19:** Amharic text wrapping and font support are included in
    the test plan.
-   [ ] **D-20:** Image optimization and reduced-motion considerations
    are documented.

## 4. Client content and approval dependencies

-   [ ] **D-21:** Adamu confirms the public service list and coverage
    area.
-   [ ] **D-22:** Phone and WhatsApp contact details are verified for
    public use.
-   [ ] **D-23:** Adamu supplies or approves real project images and
    descriptions.
-   [ ] **D-24:** Testimonials and certificates are verified and
    approved for publication.
-   [ ] **D-25:** English and Amharic wording is reviewed before
    implementation.
-   [ ] **D-26:** The inquiry form submission destination is chosen.
-   [ ] **D-27:** The final logo concept and tagline are approved by the
    client.

## 5. Phase exit decision

Phase 2 may be marked complete when: 1. All design documentation is
complete. 2. The client approves the brand direction, sitemap, and page
specifications. 3. Open content dependencies are documented with owners.
4. The MVP remains within the five-page scope. 5. The design acceptance
checklist is reviewed and signed off.

**Next phase:** Phase 3 --- Repository and Development Environment
Setup. Do not begin coding until Phase 2 is approved.
